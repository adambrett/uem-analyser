Universal Eating Monitor Analyser

Upload Universal Eating Monitor text files, choose the detected VAS questions to include, and download one XLSX workbook per participant, or a ZIP when multiple workbooks are produced. Each session becomes a worksheet with take, refill, pause, minute, quartile, raw VAS, and interpolated VAS analysis.

Processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded, never leave your computer, and are not stored anywhere.

You can select one or more .txt exports. Files must be 5MB or smaller.
